=== tuition ===
**Tuition**: EURO 2.200<strong>→</strong> 2.700

The tuition is following a **sliding scale** approach. The sliding scale wants to acknowledge the fact that the “value” of money can change widely depending of many factors. Participants from different countries and with different backgrounds, may have different access to income or to financial support. In a highly international training, different participants will also have very different travel costs.

If you can and want to pay on the higher end of the scale, you will be supporting the collective field of practice and the social and cultural diversity of the class, as well as funding Helikos School and Giovanni’s ongoing pedagogic research. Please indicate in your application the amount that you choose to pay.  
<br>
A limited number of ‘reduced tuition scholarships’ are available for this course. They are offered specifically to participants coming from countries with weaker currencies in relationship with the Euro/US$/AUD. It is up to you to identify if you think you are in need of a reduced price. If you are interested in this option please outline it in your letter of motivation.  
<br>
Please let us know if you need any specific certificate or letter of support in order to apply for some form of funding or grant.  
<br>
Please note that the tuition does not include accommodation and that participants will be responsible for organizing their staying in Padova.

Here is a list of useful information and contacts on how to organize your staying in Padova : [](/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/welcome.to_.padova-2024.pdf)**[WELCOME TO PADOVA](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/welcome.to_.padova.2026.pdf)**.
=== applications ===
There is no application form.  
To apply to **THE RED NOSE INTENSIVE,** please email  
<br>
– ***a short bio***, including a recent photograph  
<br>
– ***a letter of motivation*** sharing the essence of your story, vision, dream, desire, fear or inexplicable intuition that leads you to this training.  
<br>
Please include in your letter  
\-how did you come to know about this work;  
\-your experience with movement based theatre;  
\-your previous experience with clown;  
\-for what purpose you apply to this program;  
\-some notes about your personal journey in terms of healing or therapeutic practices, or other relevant inner experiences;  
– anything about your physical or emotional state that you feel it is important for the teacher to know (ex: injuries or preexisting physical conditions; specific physical or emotional needs; psychological states, diagnosed or un-diagnosed).

If you are thinking about asking AI to write for you… please read [](/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/a-note-about-ai-1.pdf)[**this note**](/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/a-note-about-ai.pdf) first.

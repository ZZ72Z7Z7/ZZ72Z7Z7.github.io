<p class="sign-name">GIOVANNI FUSETTI</p>
<p class="sign-roles">Artist, Natural Scientist, Pedagogue<br>Multi-Disciplinary Fool</p>

I first encountered theater while pursuing a degree in Agriculture and Ecology at the University of Padova, where I studied from 1985-1989. This started me on an artistic and pedagogic quest that has now been unfolding for over three decades.

After attending a variety of workshops and projects involving Mime, Puppets, Street Theatre, Theatre of the Oppressed, Community Theatre, Storytelling and Clown, I trained at the École Internationale de Théâtre Jacques Lecoq from 1992-1994, and returned in 1997 to follow the pedagogic training (La Troisième Année Pedagogique) and the L.E.M. (Laboratoire d’Etude du Mouvement), after which I was invited by Lecoq to teach Improvisation at the school. While in Paris, I also trained in Gestalt Therapy at the Ecole Parisienne de Gestalt (1992-1999).

After Lecoq’s death in 1999, I returned to Padua and alongside Paola Coletto co-founded Kiklos Teatro, an international theatre center devoted to theatre creation and pedagogy. I served as director for the Scuola Internazionale di Creazione at KIKLOS for five years, developing the vision of a school both rooted in lineage and free to create and re-create new poetic paths.

<figure class="bio-photo"><img src="/images/gio-kiklos.jpg" width="1024" height="867" alt="Black-and-white photograph of a group of performers on a dark stage, smiling and waving, with a young man in a dark vest at the centre" loading="lazy"></figure>

After the end of the Kiklos experience in 2004, I continued to work internationally as a teacher, lecturer, pedagogue, and director with leading theater companies, universities, schools and training centers in the field of Movement Based Theater, including LISPA (London International School of Performing Arts), Dell’Arte School of Physical Theatre (Blue Lake, California), Cabuia Teatro (Buenos Aires-Argentina), Pig Iron School for Advanced Performance Training (USA), Embodied Poetics (London UK), IIVVSS International Institute of Very Very Serious Studies (Beirut, Lebanon), Scuola di Teatro all’Avogaria (Venice), Accademia Teatrale Veneta (Venice), The University of London, Rosegarden Theatre House (Norway), Naropa University (Boulder, Colorado), Centre Generation Tao (Paris), Princeton University, University of Auckland (NZ), Auckland Unitec (NZ), Toi Wakhaari / New Zealand Drama School (NZ), The University of Colorado (USA), Victoria College of the Arts, (Australia), Stockholm University of the Arts (Sweden).

I have also worked as a trainer of Hospital Clowns with Fondazione Thedora (ITALY), New Zealand Clown Doctors, The Humor Foundation (Australia), RDEČI NOSKI (Slovenia), Klinikclowns, (Germany), Red Nose International (Austria).

In 2010, I founded the HELIKOS International School of Theatre Creation in Florence, Italy, serving as both the pedagogical director and core instructor for a three-year training program, teaching Movement Analysis, Improvisation, Theatre Creation and the PAPAS atelier (Personal Awareness Process in Altered States). In 2015 Helikos moved to Padova, Italy where it continues to offer international trainings and artistic residencies.

<figure class="bio-photo"><img src="/images/gio-masks.jpg" width="1024" height="1533" alt="A man with dark hair and round glasses smiles down at two terracotta-coloured theatre masks that he holds in front of him" loading="lazy"></figure>

As a performer, I have explored movement-based theatre creation with various companies including Circo Crappo, Teatro Osvaldo, The Clod Ensemble (London), Tre Magi Teatro, Il Triangelo, and Larven Teatro. I have worked as movement coach and assistant director for the Commedia dell’Arte company Pantakin da Venezia, and have collaborated with Pig Iron Theatre Company in Philadelphia (US) as trainer and devising consultant. I have directed and co-written clown and bouffons shows with former students and alumni. Recent collaborations include my artistic and pedagogic work with Kimberley Twiner, at the Melbourne Physical Theatre School.

Currently I focus mainly on pedagogic practice, research, and transmission. My process integrates movement-based theater with a diverse array of practices in physical and emotional awareness, including Gestalt Therapy, Reichian Analysis, Bioenergetics and Process Work, and draws on my training in Conflict Resolution as well as my background in Natural Sciences and Ecology. I explore the ancient art of theatre as a path to individual and collective ecstasy.

In addition to training professional theatre artists, I have worked with children of all ages, homeless populations, activists, teachers, and therapists, using theatre as a pedagogic paradigm of personal healing and poetic awakening. Red Nose Clown holds a special space in my heart and work: I am a passionate "clown midwife" and have coached hundreds of clowns into existence over the past 30+ years, both in professional trainings and clown-based healing processes (Tao of Clown, Clown and Gestalt).

I am deeply grateful to all the masters and teachers who have opened the path before me, and to all the students who have offered me inspiration and enrichment along the way. I am also a trained Permaculture Designer, amateur gardener and ecstatic mushroom hunter, seeking to be fully alive in my time on this powerful, fragile and beautiful planet.

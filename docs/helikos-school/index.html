Helikos School

Helikos International School of Theatre Creation, Italy

Helikos International School of Theatre Creation was founded in Florence, Italy by Giovanni Fusetti.  

​Between 2010 and 2014, Helikos welcomed students from all over the world to participate in an advanced professional training in theatre creation and movement theatre, in the form of two Three-Year Programs (2010/2013 & 2013/2014) and a One-Year Program (2013/2014).

After 4 years of activity in Florence, at the end of the 2013/2014 season, Helikos stopped offering the long term programs in Italy. In 2019 Helikos opened a studio in Padova, and offered a three month Pedagogic Training, The Pedagogic Journey. A second session of this training happened in 2025. Since its move to Padova Helikos as hosted a variety of workshops, and devising residencies, including four sessions of the RED NOSE INTENSIVE. For further details you can visit the page of the past workshops.

A new long term program is in the process of being devised and planned.

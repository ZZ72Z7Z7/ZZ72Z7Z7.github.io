<li class="entry-card sched ev show has-thumb" data-place="FunnyBusiness Company, Auckland, NZ" data-date="2016 - 2024"><div class="entry-left"><h2 class="entry-title">Mockingbird</h2><p class="entry-sub">A black comedy about mental health</p><img class="entry-thumb" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/mockingbird.png" alt="" loading="lazy"><div class="show-credits"><p><strong>Co-directed by</strong><br>Ruth Dudding and Giovanni Fusetti</p><p><strong>Written by</strong> Lisa Brickell<br><strong>Composer</strong> Sarah Macombee<br><strong>Dramaturge</strong> Ruth Dudding</p><p><strong>Produced by:</strong> <a href="http://www.funny-business.co.nz">Funny Business</a></p></div></div><div class="entry-right"><details class="show-more"><summary><span class="sm-more">Read more</span><span class="sm-less">Read less</span></summary><div class="show-body"><div class="show-desc"><p>Based on the true stories of four generations of women and their devilish alter egos, <em>Mockingbird</em> explores postnatal distress, which affects approximately one in seven women and many men every year.</p><p>Written by Lisa Brickell in collaboration with composer Sarah Macombee and dramaturge Ruth Dudding, <em>Mockingbird</em> is performed by Lecoq trained actor Lisa Brickell and musician/actor Siri Embla. Directed by Giovanni Fusetti and Ruth Dudding, <em>Mockingbird</em> follows Tina (Brickell), a counsellor who is afraid of commitment and terrified of having children due to her family history of postnatal depression. Brickell masterfully switches between four generations of characters and their hilarious ‘inner critic’ which she performs in a <em>Commedia dell’arte</em> style half mask.</p></div><div class="show-media"><ul class="show-links"><li><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ayV6ap70-cM&amp;t=50s" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Video Trailer</a></li></ul></div></div></details></div></li>

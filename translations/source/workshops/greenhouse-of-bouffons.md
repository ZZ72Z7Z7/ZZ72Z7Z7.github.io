=== subtitle ===
A devising Intensive for Bouffons and other Satyrical Creatures
=== body ===
**A unique combination of devising and training  
specifically devised to support performers to work on their own material**

In order to create and write from one’s own bouffon state, it is necessary to understand what lies at its comic core: which are the founding principles of Bouffons Satyrical Play ? The “mechanisms” of this particular form of theatre making?

As a theatre genre and a poetic territory, *Bouffon* works in a very specific way. In order to devise with its comic rules and dynamics, the actor-player needs a sharp understanding of their nature.

Once the devising process starts shifting from improvisation and playing towards writing and directing, this knowledge is crucial.

### **THE PROGRAM**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/bouf.pax_.group_.jpg)
<br>

This workshop is designed as a devising laboratory to support the projects of the participants.

Participants will bring their Bouffon creature/s, and one existing act, routine or project, solo or not, finished or unfinished. They will present their work on the first day (max. 15 minutes), and this will be the basis for a series of coaching sessions that will focus on the three different hats that the actor/creator needs to wear and their areas of competence, responsibility and choice making.

**THE PLAYER /PERFORMER**  

-   The Bouffon State
-   The Bouffon Body
-   The Player’s technique: *le jeu,* amplification, repetition and ritualization
-   Articulation of movement and levels of playing
-   The contact with the audience
-   The Mocking eyes
-   The shapeshifting between the Bouffon and the Roles/Characters that they play

**THE DIRECTOR**  /**STAGER**

-   Playing in space and with space
-   Rhythm: pace, crescendo, suspension and attacks
-   The structure of an bouffon act: arrival of the Bouffons, arrival of the theme, development and variations in the mocking cycle, crescendo, climax, finale, exit
-   Costume and props
-   Directing the Player

**THE WRITER** /**AUTHOR**

-   The poetic field of Bouffon in relationship with Tragedy
-   The choice of themes and their development
-   Understanding Satire
-   The dynamic between the author and the Bouffon
-   The Bouffon as a non-linear story-teller
-   The “politics” of the act

### **THE DIFFERENT SESSIONS**

The aim of this training is to give participants professional theatre tools, in order to create within their own poetic world. It addresses theatre artists with experience in the world of Bouffons. Participants need to bring an existing project, something they have already worked with and that can be performed on the first day.  
<br>
The workshop will unfold with different kinds of sessions:

– **Presentation of the work to the group and collective work on the feedback,** to understand basic principles of playing, writing and directing.

**– Individual coaching with Giovanni**

**– Autonomous rehearsal time**

– **Collective exercises** on core principles emerging from the work

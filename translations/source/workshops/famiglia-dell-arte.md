**A PLAYFUL JOURNEY OF PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT**

Welcome to a highly experiential and playful group of personal development in which the *Psychodrama Approach* and *Physical Theatre* will dance together to provide a unique experience of emotional awareness and embodied spontaneity.  
<br>
The focus of the group will be on the exploration of the Family as a field of roles, dynamics and relational patterns.

The Family of origin is the founding ground of our psychological development, a field of ancestral unfolding in which we often find the origin of many of our core psychological patterns. The family, in its inevitable intimate intensity, is a field of great power and great paradoxes: love and hurt, nurturing and wounding, support and conflicts, belonging and individuation. Emotions of all kinds flow under the family roof and the deep creative nature of this wonderful human configuration always dances with the potential for great heartbreaks. It can provide lifelong resources and support or endless disappointments and painful acceptance, as well as the possibility of healing, reparation and transformation.  
<br>
A family is started by individuals with specific personalities and gives birth and nurtures other personalities, deeply entangled and intertwined with each other. Sometimes in very faithful reproduction, sometimes in fierce opposition, very often in both.  
<br>
A family is also a micro manifestation of the society, in which specific cultures, values, and archetypal roles are in play. And in the background there is the world history with its collective events, and the deep mystery and awe of human condition. The story of each individual family is a piece of the story of the human family!  
<br>
The workshop proposes a journey exploring how the family dynamics manifest in the life of an individual. The family of origin, the current family, the future family that a person can wish to create.

The ***Psychodrama approach*** allows the exploration of figures and roles of the present, as well as beloved and be-hated ones who have passed, ancestors and unborn children of the future. Through the perspective of Theatre each family life can be seen as a very elaborate play, in which each role follows some existential and psychological trajectories that often look like scripts of a play. In every family there is drama and melodrama, comedy and tragedy, heroes and fools, victims and oppressors…. A whole world to explore through play!

The family was a central theme of the Greek tragedies and it is still a fundamental  subject of countless novels, movies, and TV-series. It is impossible to bypass it!

In a Psychodrama the protagonist is encouraged to stage their family, past, present or future, and explore it through playing the various roles and characters, as well through witnessing other participants  playing them. Say unsaid messages, address unfinished gestalts, meet with ancestors or unborn children.

***Physical theater*** brings embodiment, amplification and spontaneity to the play of the roles in the family and supports Psychodrama to manifest the fundamental theatrical nature of the psyche. In the family life highly theatrical events keep happening: conflicts, alliances and betrayals, separations, fights and reconciliations, battles and peace treaties, rites of passages, death and renewal. The rules of dramaturgy are all there! Crescendo, development of themes, amplification, sudden dramatic shifts, resolutions, dramatic peaks and happy or tragic endings.

The protagonist of the Psychodrama can stage their family field with the support of the group and witness the unfolding scripts of their psyche, seen and transposed through the lenses of the theatre genres. Tragedy, Melodrama, Satyre, Absurd and, very often, Comedy.

**TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN…**

The workshop is open to anyone wishing to engage in a quest of personal awareness and emotional awakening through role playing and theatrical exercises, within the context of a Psychodrama group.

This workshop is specifically designed for anyone interested in exploring their psychological field in an embodied and theatrical way, in which the body and its language comes first, before words. 

-   Theatre practitioners who are interested in a therapeutic process that includes play and theatricality: this can be as deeply healing as fundamentally fun.
-   Teachers, educators, therapists, psychotherapists, psychodramatists, drama-therapists and anyone active in the field of healing who is interested in exploring psychology and therapy using a theatrical paradigm based on embodiment, play and fun.
-   Anyone who is already engaged in a theatrical or therapeutic process.
-   Adventurers of the Psyche who know and feel that humor and play are great allies in the journey of becoming.

If you are coming from the field of theatre arts, you can expect to practice a therapeutic process constantly fed by the dynamics and techniques of physical theatre and embodied play.

If you are coming from the psychological field you can expect to explore emotional processes and wounds through theatrical games and exercises and the creation of characters, in collective movement practice,improvisation and within a psychodramatic re-enactment.  
<br>
A fierce and playful curiosity about human folly and beauty is required, as well as a passionate engagement to the exploration of one’s own emotional life in an artistic way.

Though no previous specific knowledge of Psychodrama and Physical Theatre are required, a previous experience in *Personal Development/Therapy* and in the space of *Theatre and Play* is necessary. 

**THE APPROACH**

The pedagogical approach integrates the practice and the experience of the two facilitators.

**Psychodrama** proposes a journey of emotional awareness and creative transformation of one’s own psychic structure, roles and relationships through the re-enactment of inner and outer life scenarios. In this sense it is a highly theatrical therapeutic practice, in which the *protagonist* stages (*enacts*) a scenario of their life using the group both as cast (*auxiliaries*) and as *audience* of the play.

If you wish to know more about Psychodrama you can visit [www.zso.fi/psychodrama](http://www.zso.fi/psychodrama)

**Physical Theatre** provides a set of exercises, games and playful techniques that support the embodiment of the different roles, thus encouraging a deeper experience of the process. The result is a deep artistic and emotional work, involving the body in a dynamic of play and spontaneity.

Theatre artists will discover a fun way to work on themselves with psychological depth and theatrical delight.

### **WRITING AND DEVISING CLOWN POETRY**

This workshop is an in-depth exploration of the poetic world of Clown, **an archetypal comic space** for the actor-creator and a key step in the journey of movement based theatre.

This advanced workshop will go into the depth of **the clown state**, considered as an **Non-Ordinary State** of deep creativity. The work starts with the analysis of the physical and emotional world of the actor, as revealed by their natural body with the help of the **Neutral State**. The reference to this state deepens the physical presence and allows a profound connection with the core movement patterns of each person. This leads to **the unfolding of a unique clown**, with a peculiar state of perception of reality.  
<br>
**The poetical power of the clown** is based on an extreme emotional openness and vulnerability, a vivid articulation of the present moment, and a very direct and naïve approach to the world. The fluidity of contact with reality is the foundation of the poetic flow.  
<br>
The Clown is a poetic transposition of the actor’s most intimate space. As the Neutral Mask is the mask of humanity, the Red Nose is the humanity of the mask.  
<br>
Once the **individual clowns** have emerged, the journey of the workshop will guide them to the exploration of themes of everyday life, like love and relationships, illness and death, power and hierarchy, social conventions and institutions such as education, politics and health care…  
<br>
The final step will be the approach to **poetic writing** and each actor will work on a clown monologue and on a variety of scenes emerged from the exploration. The final step of the workshop will be **a public performance** of the devised acts.

The last two days of the class, after the performance, are dedicated to the integration of the work and the exploration of various ways to bring the clown work on stage, with and without red nose, and in life.

### **THE PROGRAM**

**THE CLOWN “STATE”**  
– Movement Analysis and Core Physical Patterns  
– The Neutral Mask  
– From the neutral state to the comic state: movement analysis of the body in space  
– The Red Nose as a mask: amplification and articulation of movement  
– The clown as an altered state of perception  
– The poetic space and the non-intentional mind  
– Amplification of movements, patterns, emotions, clown relationships  
– The Clown’s Voice: breath, resonation, emotion, words  

**WRITING FROM THE CLOWN STATE**  
– Writing from the Emotional body of the actor  
– Emotional awareness and Poetic awareness  
– Exploring life through the eyes of clowns: power and hierarchy, love and relationships, the stages of lifes, illness and death, education and mental health, politics and spiritual awakenings…

**DEVISING CLOWN TEXTS**  
– Devising of Clown monologues and acts based on the previous improvisations  
– Staging and directing clowns: each clown will work and rework on some acts to be presented in a public performance  
– Public presentation of a *Clown’s Life Performance*, devised by the participants under the direction of Giovanni Fusetti  
– Feedback on the performance.  
– The clown as a comic archetype: all participants play each other’s clown characters  
– Feedback on the whole process and integration of the work: perspectives on the development of the red nose state on stage and in life.

**ADMISSION**  
This workshop is for theatre practitioners with previous experiences in movement-based theatre and in the Pedagogy of Jacques Lecoq.

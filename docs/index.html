I practice the freedom to be

stupidus

stupefied by life, nature, beauty
and the mystery of the human condition

I celebrate the sublime stupidity of Clowns
I long for the crazy wisdom of The Fool

intelligence is limited
stupidity is infinite

Clowning is very serious work
on how to be stupid

to the delusional quest
for a mechanical artificial intelligence
I will respond with the daily practice
of embodied natural stupidity

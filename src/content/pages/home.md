---
title: "Home"
---

<div class="poem-s">

<p class="lead">I practice the freedom to be</p>

<p class="word">stupidus</p>

<p>stupefied by life, nature, beauty<br>and the mystery of the human condition</p>

<p>I celebrate the sublime stupidity of Clowns<br>I long for the crazy wisdom of The Fool</p>

<p>intelligence is limited<br>stupidity is infinite</p>

<p>Clowning is very serious work<br>on how to be stupid</p>

<p>to the delusional quest<br>for a mechanical artificial intelligence<br>I will respond with the daily practice<br>of embodied natural stupidity</p>

</div>

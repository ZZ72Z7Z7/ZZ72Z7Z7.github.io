**EARLY BIRD TUITION**  
Sliding Scale 850 AUD to 1.000 AUD: if full payment is made before January 15th, 2027

The tuition is following a **sliding scale** approach. The sliding scale wants to acknowledge the fact that the “value” of money can change widely depending on many factors. Participants from different backgrounds, may have different access to income or to financial support. In an international training, different participants will also have very different travel costs.

If you can and want to pay on the higher end of the scale, you will be supporting the collective field of practice and the social and cultural diversity of the class, as well as funding our work and our ongoing pedagogic research. Please indicate in your application the amount that you choose to pay.

=== applications ===
To apply to the workshop please e-mail a letter of motivation (in English) to Giovanni and Zsófi at **[kairosplay@protonmail.com](mailto:kairosplay@protonmail.com)** sharing what brings you to return to this work.

Please mention in your letter what was the essence of your experience in the previous workshop(s) with Zsófi and Giovanni and what specific theme(s) of your life you would like to bring to this new workshop.  
<br>
Before applying please read the document **[The Rules of Engagement](/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/rules-of-engagement.advanced-mel-3.pdf)**, that you can find clicking on it. It contains:  
– the rules of engagement of the participants with the workshop: with oneself, with the group, with the teacher and with the work;  
– the guidelines on what to include in the motivation letter;  
– our cancellation and refund policy.
